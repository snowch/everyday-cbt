How you’ll learn

A little understanding.
Something to try.

Follow fictional characters through familiar moments: an unanswered message, a difficult week, a conversation that keeps replaying. Explore what might keep a pattern going, then choose a manageable next step.

The aim is careful observation, balanced reasoning, and useful action. Sometimes that means reconsidering a thought. Sometimes it means solving a real problem, setting a boundary, or asking for support.

  1. Meet the situation
  2. Explore the pattern
  3. Try a small experiment
  4. Reflect on what happened

Where things stand

A draft you can explore.

The twelve lessons, recorded narration, lab workbook, and independent cases are available. The course is a draft; no qualified practitioner review or learner pilot has taken place.

Use fictional examples throughout if you prefer. This is introductory skills education, not diagnosis or individual treatment. The linked guidance explains the course’s scope, sources, and how progress and writing are handled.
