How you’ll learn

A little understanding.
Something to try.

Follow fictional characters through familiar moments: an unanswered message, a difficult week, a conversation that keeps replaying. Explore what might keep a pattern going, then choose a manageable next step.

The aim is careful observation, balanced reasoning, and useful action. Sometimes that means reconsidering a thought. Sometimes it means solving a real problem, setting a boundary, or asking for support.

  1. Meet the situation
  2. Explore the pattern
  3. Try a small experiment
  4. Reflect on what happened

The planned curriculum

From noticing to practising.

Start with the connections. Build a set of skills. Bring them together in situations that don’t have a tidy answer.

Where things stand

The course starts here.

This is the course plan. Lessons, narration, worksheets, and learning checks are still to be developed. The estimated time and format may change as the lessons are tested.

The intended scope is introductory CBT skills education. It does not provide diagnosis or individual treatment. Clinical review and a learner pilot are planned; neither has taken place yet.
