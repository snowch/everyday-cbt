Design reference
----------------
At the repository owner's request, the visual design follows Common Ground's
field-guide styling: pale blue paper, dark blue cover, Libre Baskerville headings,
Arimo text, ocean/brass accents, and numbered chapter rows.
Reference: https://github.com/snowch/common-ground-social-psychology
No Common Ground lesson text, narration, or illustrations are bundled here.
The CBT diagram and page implementation were adapted within this repository.

Narration production tools
--------------------------
Recordings were generated with Kokoro-82M v1.0 via kokoro-onnx 0.6.1 using
the af_heart synthetic voice. Model binaries and production dependencies are not
bundled in the course. Production URLs, model hashes, settings, and review limits
are in production/narration.json and production/README.md. Recordings are original
readings of this course; no Common Ground recordings are reused.
